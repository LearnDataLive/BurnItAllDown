## Plan: Simultaneous 5-Language Product Launch

Goal: Launch v2 of the product in English, German, French, Spanish, and Japanese at the same time.

Fixed constraints:
1. All 5 language versions must go live on the same launch date -- no staggering.
2. Translation must be done by human translators, no machine translation, per brand guidelines.
3. Marketing has already booked a hard launch date 6 weeks out and paid for press coverage.
4. Each language's UI must be pixel-reviewed by a native-speaking designer before launch.
5. Engineering headcount for localization work is 1 person, part-time (50%).
